V6 auto-calibration is enabled for four-side support. The 6 mm monolithic surface is calibrated exactly to three known source points: ASTM A3.1 (1200×1500 → 2.50 kPa), ASTM A3.3 (1520×1900 → 1.80 kPa), and the supplied report (2000×2800 → 1.00 kPa). Laminated 8 mm is additionally calibrated to ASTM A3.3 (1520×1900 → 2.50 kPa). Other thicknesses are interpolated from ASTM Table 4 minimum thicknesses and their correct Annex A1 chart family.
